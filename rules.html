📜 Server Rules

⚔️
PvP Rules
PvP is enabled and encouraged! Fight in designated areas. No PvP in spawn zones. Respect other players and keep fights fair. Teaming is allowed but excessive camping is not.
🚫
No Griefing
Griefing is strictly prohibited. Protect your builds with claims or trusted players. Grief will result in immediate punishment and potential ban. Your base is your responsibility.
💬
Chat Rules
Keep chat respectful and appropriate. No spam, hate speech, harassment, or advertising. Follow staff instructions at all times. Use appropriate language suitable for all ages.
🛡️
Anti-Cheat Policy
Hacking, cheating, and exploiting bugs are forbidden. Our anti-cheat system monitors gameplay 24/7. Violators face immediate ban. Report bugs to staff immediately.
🏗️
Building Rules
Be creative but respectful. Don't build near spawn or others' bases without permission. Keep the server lag-free by avoiding excessive entities. Claim your land properly.
👤
Account Security
Protect your account with a strong password. Don't share accounts or login info. Staff won't ask for your password. Enable 2FA on your Discord account for safety.
⏰
AFK Farming Limits
Don't AFK farm excessively. Keep gameplay active and engaging. Respect server resources. Unlimited AFK at home is okay, but not in farms or grinding areas.
🎮
Fair Play Only
Use only vanilla Minecraft client. No advantage mods or hacked clients allowed. Play fairly and enjoy the competitive spirit! Allowed: OptiFine, Sodium, shader packs.
